Google Business Profile Checklist: The Fields That Decide Your Map Ranking

Most businesses fill in their Google Business Profile once and forget it. But the profile is not a form — it is the page Google ranks. These are the fields that carry the most weight, in the order to fix them.

The checklist

1 — Primary category. The single strongest ranking signal you control. It must name the service customers search for, not the industry you feel part of. A roofer is "Roofing contractor", not "Construction company".

2 — Services, one by one. Every service listed separately, in the words customers type. Services left blank are searches you cannot appear in.

3 — Business name, address and phone — identical everywhere. The same name, same address format, same phone number on your website, your profile and every directory. Small mismatches quietly split your authority.

4 — Photos that prove the work. Real jobs, real premises, added regularly. Profiles with fresh photos earn actions — calls, directions, clicks — that stock-photo profiles do not.

5 — Hours, kept true. Holiday hours included. A "closed" surprise costs a customer and, over time, trust signals.

6 — Reviews, answered. Every review replied to, good or bad. The reply is read by the next customer, not the reviewer.
